# Dungeon Tile Types and Procedures

Below is a list of the **12 distinct tile (chamber) types** in the game, each governed by its own specific rule or procedure when entered. Tiles are grouped by their primary function to help players quickly understand their effects.

---

## **1. Card Draw Tiles**
These tiles require the player to draw a card, which may introduce new challenges, rewards, or events.

- **Dungeon Room**: Draw a Dungeon card.
- **Trap Room**: Draw a Trap card.
- **Catacomb Entrance**: Draw a Dungeon card. On your next turn, you have the option to enter the Catacombs instead of moving normally.
- **Chasm**: Draw a Dungeon card. Because an uncrossable fissure splits the tile, on your next turn you can only exit through a passage located on the same side of the fissure you entered from.
- **Cave-in**: Draw a Dungeon card. On your next turn, you must choose to either retreat to the chamber you came from or test your Agility to successfully cross the debris. Failing the test leaves you trapped there for the turn.
- **Treasure Chamber**: Located at the center of the board. You must draw a Dragon card to see if Dragonlord Kalladra awakens.

---

## **2. Movement-Based Tiles**
These tiles force the player to move or alter their movement in some way.

- **Corridor**: Forces you to immediately move again, with the restriction that you cannot enter the exact same Corridor tile twice during a single turn.
- **Chamber of Darkness**: You must immediately move again. Roll a six-sided die to randomly determine which passage you must exit through; if that passage is blocked by a wall, your turn ends there, and you must roll again next turn.
- **Rotating Room**: You must immediately rotate the tile 180 degrees upon entry, which can potentially block your path and result in a dead end.
- **Bridge**: You must decide to either stay on your entry side (requiring you to return to your previous chamber on your next turn) or attempt to cross the bridge by testing Agility with a +1 penalty/modifier added to your dice roll.

---

## **3. Luck/Stat Test Tiles**
These tiles require the player to test their Luck or Agility, often with high stakes.

- **Bottomless Pit**: Test Luck. If you succeed, you cross safely; if you fail, your Hero falls and is killed.
- **Bridge**: You must decide to either stay on your entry side or attempt to cross the bridge by testing Agility with a +1 penalty/modifier added to your dice roll.
- **Cave-in**: On your next turn, you must choose to either retreat to the chamber you came from or test your Agility to successfully cross the debris. Failing the test leaves you trapped there for the turn.

---

## **4. Special/Location-Dependent Tiles**
These tiles have unique rules based on their location on the board or special conditions.

- **Tower Room**: Located at the corners of the board. If you possess one or more Loot cards, you may choose to exit and win/survive the dungeon; otherwise, you must immediately move out of it.
- **Treasure Chamber**: Located at the center of the board. You must draw a Dragon card to see if Dragonlord Kalladra awakens.
